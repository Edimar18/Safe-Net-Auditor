The root cause is clear now. Looking at your project context — the Consumer<NetworkProvider> in the screens isn't the problem, IndexedStack isn't the problem either. The real issue is that Consumer uses Provider.of(context, listen: true) internally, which registers the widget as a dependent of the InheritedWidget. But with async data flowing in from serial, something in the Provider/element reconciliation isn't scheduling repaints for those widgets.
The header works because it's a StatefulWidget with its own state cycle. The fix: give every screen explicit addListener → setState control, bypassing Consumer entirely. When notifyListeners() fires, we call setState(() {}) at the screen level ourselves — guaranteed, every time.
